* 🛡️ **Core Infrastructure & Sovereign Utilities:** Privacy-by-design foundational services including `Ind.Browser`, `Ind.Mail`, and community networks via [ind.center](https://ind.center).
* 📊 **Domain Verticals:**
  * **EdTech & Discovery:** [ind.shiksha](https://ind.shiksha) & [ind.quest](https://ind.quest)
  * **Finance, Trading & Analytics:** [ind.trading](https://ind.trading) & [ind.report](https://ind.report)
  * **Lifestyle & Services:** [ind.run](https://ind.run) & [ind.pet](https://ind.pet)
* 🖥️ **Ind.OS (Long-term Milestone):** A sovereign, privacy-first operating system designed as a secure bridge between hardware and Indian end-users.
* 📦 **Productivity & Utilities:** Unified alternatives across `Ind.Docs`, `Ind.Pay`, `Ind.Health`, and `Ind.Maps`.
